## What Are Kei Fire Trucks?

Kei fire trucks (軽消防車, *kei shōbōsha*) are compact fire suppression vehicles built on Japan's
**kei-class** (軽自動車) platform — vehicles with engine displacements of 660cc or less, designed
to navigate the narrow roads of rural and mountainous Japan.

These vehicles are widely used by Japan's volunteer fire brigades (*shōbō-dan*, 消防団) and are
increasingly found internationally, prized for their maneuverability and reliability.
