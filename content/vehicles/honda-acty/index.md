## Overview

The Honda Acty (*アクティ*) is a long-running kei truck platform that served as a popular base
for light fire apparatus throughout Japan. Spanning several generations from 1977 to 2021, the
Acty was valued for its reliable **E07Z** three-cylinder engine and compact dimensions.

The fire truck variants are predominantly conversion bodies built by specialist manufacturers
such as Morita, Tohatsu, and Ishikawajima, fitted to the standard Acty truck chassis.

## Generation Guide

| Chassis | Years | Wheelbase | Engine |
|---------|-------|-----------|--------|
| HA3/HA4 | 1990–1999 | 1,810 mm | E07A/E07Z |
| HA6/HA7 | 1999–2009 | 1,810 mm | E07Z |
| HA8/HA9 | 2009–2021 | 1,940 mm | E07Z (fuel-injected) |

## Notes for Importers

The HA8/HA9 generation has a longer wheelbase than earlier variants — confirm which generation
your vehicle is before sourcing body parts or replacement hose guides. The E07Z engine is
well-supported with aftermarket parts globally.
